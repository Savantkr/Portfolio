Execution Engine: keeping people in control of a running robot workflow.

Role
Senior Staff Product Designer
Scope
Execution page, run and node controls, video, PDF report
Team
[TEAM]
Timeline
[TIMELINE]

01 · Context

Lab teams register their devices — IoT sensors, homogenizers, balances, cobots — and then build automations at the workspace level: pick a trigger (manual, webhook or schedule), chain device-action nodes, configure each one.

Once a run started, the execution page offered stop, pause and cancel. That was it. An invalid configuration halfway through left one honest option: abandon the whole run.

02 · Intervening without restarting

I mapped what an operator actually needs to do to a live run, then split the controls by where they act: on the run as a whole, or on a single node.

NodePause at nodeHold the run at a chosen step instead of wherever it happens to be.
NodeRetryRun a failed step again when the cause was transient.
NodeRetry with corrected inputFix an invalid configuration in place and continue the same run.
NodeSkip nodeMove past a step that isn’t needed for this run.
NodeBreakpointMark a node so the run stops there for inspection.

Cut from scope on purpose: force complete, and approval sign-off gates — both belong somewhere other than a live execution screen.

03 · Progress without a step count

Real automations branch, loop, wait on conditions and fan out into five or more parallel threads that may never rejoin. “Step 7 of 12” stops meaning anything.

So the progress view drops the total. Nodes appear one by one as they run, and each active branch shows only its next node — what’s happening, and what happens next, per thread.

04 · Seeing the run

Cameras attached to devices or to the whole workcell stream live during execution. After completion, a player replays the run with step markers placed automatically; recordings also land on the Videos page.

Review pushed back: with many cameras, was the panel relevant, would auto-follow really work, and did it feel bolted on? That moved camera choice out of the execution page and into a Video Recorder node, chosen while building the automation — and opened a new round of layout iterations.

05 · The report on paper

Every execution produces a PDF record. The redesign had two jobs the old one didn’t: make parallel paths readable without an interactive graph, and make non-success states — errors, stops, condition outcomes — impossible to miss.
